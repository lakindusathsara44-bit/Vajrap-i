Biblical Kings of Israel

The Herodian Dynasty of Judea

The Herodian family ruled different parts of the region under Roman authority. Not every member held the formal title “king”: Archelaus was an ethnarch, while Antipas and Philip were tetrarchs. Their exact territories and dates changed over time.
